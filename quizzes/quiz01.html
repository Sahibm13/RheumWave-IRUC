Pre-Quiz

This 20-question quiz is designed to assess your current knowledge and interpretation of musculoskeletal ultrasound (MSKUS) findings encountered in rheumatology practice. Results will help us evaluate MSKUS education through CRUS, identify areas where additional teaching may be helpful, help develop resources, and assess changes in knowledge over time.

Your responses are anonymous and will be pooled. They will not be used to determine successful completion or certification of the CRUS course or otherwise affect your training. Anonymized data may also be used for research and scholarly evaluation of MSKUS education in the future.

  • First you will answer 7 short questions about yourself. Those are not counted towards your time.
  • The quiz itself is timed.
  • Please complete it independently and to the best of your current knowledge. Please do not use any resources or AI to assist you.
  • Every question is on one page. Answer in any order and submit at the bottom.
  • You will not be told whether an answer is right as you go, and the correct answers are not shown afterward.
  • You may sit this quiz once. There is no retry.
  • If the page closes by accident, reopen it with the same participant ID — your answers come back and the clock carries on from when you started, so do not leave it open and walk away.
